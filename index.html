Privacy Policy for BabyTouchLock

Last updated: November 2026

BabyTouchLock is developed by Tawheed Wahid.

Information We Collect

BabyTouchLock does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device with no internet connection required.

Data Stored Locally

The app stores only the following on your device locally:

  • A 4-digit PIN you set yourself (stored in app private storage, never transmitted)
  • Your overlay tint preference

This data never leaves your device.

Permissions Used

  • Display over other apps — to draw a transparent touch-blocking overlay during video calls
  • Foreground service — to keep the overlay active while you use other apps
  • Post notifications — to show the persistent unlock notification

No permission is used to collect or transmit any data.

Third Parties

This app does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting tools.

Children

This app is intended for use by parents/adults only. It does not target children.
